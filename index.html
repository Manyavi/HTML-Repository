Sai Vidya Institute of Technology

Sai Vidya Institute of Technology (SVIT) is an autonomous engineering college located in Rajan Kunte, Bengaluru, Karnataka. Established in the year 2008 by the Sri Sai Vidya Vikas Shikshana Samithi, the institution was founded by a team of eminent academicians who envisioned a center for excellence in technical education. SVIT is affiliated with the Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), and recognized by the Government of Karnataka. The college offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctorate programs in engineering and management fields. With a core mission to blend academic brilliance with ethical values, the institute is highly regarded for its highly experienced faculty, strong placement support, and modern infrastructure. The green campus features advanced laboratories, a vast digital library, vibrant student clubs, and research hubs like the Makers Space Lab. SVIT continues to play a pivotal role in shaping competent, industry-ready professionals equipped to solve global technological challenges.
